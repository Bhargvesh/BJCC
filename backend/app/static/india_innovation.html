System Architecture

How the assistant processes speech, text, translation, and legal reasoning (as proposed).

1) Speech & Text Processing Layer (Bhashini APIs)

  • Speech-to-Text (ASR): converts courtroom proceedings into text
  • Real-Time Translation: translates content into regional languages (e.g., Hindi, Dogri)
  • Text-to-Speech (TTS): enables audio playback for accessibility

2) Legal Understanding & Summarization Engine

  • Plain-language summaries of case proceedings
  • Entity extraction (case type, parties involved, judgments)
  • ELI5 interpretations of complex legal jargon

3) Context-Aware Legal Assistance Agent

  • “What is the status of my case?”
  • “What does this judgment mean?”
  • “What are my next legal steps?”

The system provides contextual, multilingual responses, bridging the gap between legal complexity and citizen understanding.

4) Judicial Dashboard & Integration Layer

  • Web/mobile interface for courts and citizens
  • Real-time translated transcripts
  • Case summaries & highlights
  • Searchable legal records
  • e-Courts integration for seamless adoption
